Colour

Violet is the brand. Lime is a signal — it marks the thing you want clicked or read, never a decoration. Pale lime is the quieter version used for eyebrows, highlighter and the ticker. Gold appears only in star ratings.

Voice & copy rules

These are non-negotiable — they're what keeps the brand sounding like a business rather than an agency brochure.

  • Headings are objective and literal. A heading says what the section is: "Our digital marketing services", "Client results", "What are you looking to achieve?"
  • No wordplay headings. "Four services. One system." / "The proof's in the pipeline." — these get rejected every time.
  • The descriptive line is the heading. The eyebrow is the boring category label above it ("Get started", "Services", "Results"). Never swap them.
  • Use the client's exact words when they supply headings, labels or list items. Don't rewrite them into something punchier.
  • Plain over clever. Short declaratives. No jargon, no filler, no reach reports.
  • Every claim must be defensible. If a number can't be backed, cut it rather than soften it.

Brand marks

Three geometric marks, always in lime or pale lime, always breaking a container corner. One or two per section — they lose their charm in a crowd.

Layout & rhythm

One container, one section padding, one gutter. Everything else is a grid inside .dk-wrap.

  • Max width 1280px — --dk-max, applied by .dk-wrap.
  • Gutter clamp(20px, 5vw, 72px) — never a fixed value.
  • Section padding clamp(84px, 10.5vw, 152px) via .dk-band.
  • Every size scales with clamp(). No breakpoint-only font sizes.
  • Wide content scrolls inside itself — the page body never scrolls sideways.

Motion

One curve, three durations. Motion confirms an action or reveals content — it never decorates.

  • Curve cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1) for everything.
  • Durations .22s hover · .3s cards · .6s images and reveals.
  • Hover lift is translateY(-5px) on cards, -2px on buttons. Never more.
  • Reveals — add .dk-rv, toggle .is-in with an IntersectionObserver.
  • Reduced motion is honoured globally — the system disables animation and shows revealed states immediately.
const io = new IntersectionObserver(es => es.forEach(e => {
  if (!e.isIntersecting) return;
  e.target.classList.add('is-in');
  io.unobserve(e.target);
}), { rootMargin: '0px 0px -10% 0px', threshold: .12 });
document.querySelectorAll('.dk-rv').forEach(el => io.observe(el));

  • .dk on the body sets the base font, colour and ground.
  • Section = .dk-band + a ground class, content inside .dk-wrap.
  • Fonts are embedded in dunk-fonts.css — no network request, no FOUT.
  • To rebrand, override the tokens in :root. Nothing else needs touching.
