02 Why this usually goes wrong

Most agencies sell you a channel.

The channel is the last decision, not the first. Everything that goes wrong for the small businesses who come to us starts with that order being reversed.

01

You’re asked to diagnose yourself

Choosing between SEO, Google and Meta is the judgement you are trying to buy. Being asked to make it first, on a sales call, is how businesses lose a year.

02

Four suppliers, four stories

Each reports on its own channel and each one is winning. Nobody owns the number that matters, and the gaps between them are where the budget leaks.

03

Activity dressed as progress

Impressions, rankings, reach, reports you could not read. None of it is an enquiry and none of it is revenue.
