Can you guarantee the coverage or the links?

No, and neither can anyone who says otherwise. We can guarantee the work — the research, the content, the outreach and the follow-up — but whether a journalist runs the story depends on editorial interest, timing and how newsworthy it is that week. The projected outcomes on each package are what campaigns of that size typically return.

How is this different from buying links?

A bought link is placed because you paid for it, which is exactly what Google penalises. An earned link exists because a journalist thought the story was worth covering. The work is harder and slower, and it is the only version that holds up.

How long before we see anything?

Outreach usually starts landing in the first three to four weeks, and the ranking effect follows the links a month or two after that. Authority compounds, so the campaigns that look best are the ones you look back on rather than watch live.

Which publications can you actually get into?

The network includes Forbes, Entrepreneur, Harper’s Bazaar, OK Magazine, Yahoo and Green Matters, alongside Australian titles like Domain and Broadsheet. Which of them fits depends entirely on the story — we will tell you where we think it can land before you commit.

Do I need SEO running as well?

Not a requirement, but PR earns links to pages, and those links do more when the pages they point at are worth ranking. If the technical foundation is broken we would rather fix that first than send authority into a site that cannot use it.

Is the media placement fee included?

Outreach and earned coverage are included. Sponsored or paid placements are a separate cost, quoted per publication, and we will always tell you which is which.
