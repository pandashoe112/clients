How we optimise for AI Search

Search is being answered, not just listed. The work that gets you into an AI answer overlaps with what always won the blue link — but only if it is done deliberately, and measured differently.

  • Structured data an answer engine can read

    Schema on every page type, entities Google can resolve to your business rather than guess at, and internal links that make the relationships explicit.

  • Pages that answer the question directly

    An AI answer is assembled from sources that state the answer plainly. We write the direct answer first and the supporting detail under it, not the other way round.

  • Cited by the answer, not buried under it

    Being the source an answer quotes needs authority behind the claim: original detail, real specifics, and links from places that already have standing.

  • Tracked where the traffic actually goes

    Zero-click answers change what a good month looks like. We report on the queries you appear in and the enquiries that follow, not just positions.

Questions people ask before starting SEO

If yours is not here, ask it on the call. We would rather answer it before you commit to anything than after.

How long before SEO does anything?

Technical fixes can move things in weeks. New pages ranking for anything competitive is three to six months, and the compounding happens after that. Anyone promising page one in a month is either bidding on your brand name or about to get you penalised.

What does SEO cost?

SEO is quoted on the work involved rather than a flat retainer, because a five-page trade site and a thousand-SKU store are not the same job. The audit tells us the scope, and the quote comes with the reasoning attached.

How long do I need to commit to?

We recommend six to nine months. SEO needs runway: the technical work and the first content go in early and the compounding happens after that, so a three-month test tells you very little. Best practice also keeps moving, which is why we refine the campaign rather than setting it and forgetting it — many of our clients have been with us five years or more. We will say honestly if your budget and timeline do not add up before you sign anything.

Who actually does the work?

The person you meet on the call. There is no handover to a junior after you sign, and nothing is offshored to a content mill.

Do I own the work if I leave?

Yes. Your site, your analytics, your Google Business Profile, your content, your links. We work in your accounts, not ours, and we hand over everything.

Should I be doing SEO or Google Ads?

Usually both, in that order of patience. Ads buy you enquiries this week; SEO builds the traffic you are not paying for next year. If you can only run one, we will tell you which on the call.
