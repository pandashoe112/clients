Privacy policy

What we collect when you use this site or send us an enquiry, why we hold it, and how to have it removed.

Who we are

This site is operated by DUNK Agency, Level 5, 171 Collins Street, Melbourne VIC 3000, Australia. You can reach us at hello@dunk.agency or 03 9699 4585. We are the data controller for the information described here.

What we collect

Two things, and nothing else.

  • What you send us. When you fill in the enquiry form we receive your name, email, phone, company, website, revenue and marketing spend ranges, your message, and which page you sent it from. If you subscribe we receive your email address.
  • Ordinary server logs. Our host records the requests this site serves — IP address, timestamp, page, browser — as any web server does.

What we do not collect

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies, and carries no tracking pixels. Nothing here follows you to another website. Fonts are served from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests the font files from Google and Google sees the request; we receive nothing from it.

Why we hold it

To answer your enquiry, and to send the emails you asked for. That is the whole purpose. We do not sell your details, we do not share them with advertisers, and we do not add you to a mailing list because you filled in an enquiry form.

Where it goes

Enquiries are handled by Netlify Forms and reach us by email. Our email is hosted on a commercial provider. Both hold data outside Australia, which the Privacy Act permits provided we take reasonable steps to ensure comparable protection, and we have satisfied ourselves that they do.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept while there is a live conversation and for a reasonable period after, so we can pick up where we left off. Subscribers are kept until they unsubscribe. Ask us to delete either and we will.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@dunk.agency and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied with how we have handled a privacy matter you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

If this policy changes we will replace this page and the date below. We do not keep an archive of previous versions.

This policy is a plain-language summary written for a small business site. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.
