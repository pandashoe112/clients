Questions

The ones everybody asks

How long until I see something?

New terms usually start appearing within the first two weeks, because most local sites have obvious gaps that fill fast. Competitive terms take three to six months. Anyone promising page one in 30 days is selling you ads.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Month to month. Every page, fix and piece of content we produce lives on your site and stays there if you leave.

Do I need a new website?

Often not. We audit first and tell you honestly. If your site is fast and structured we work with it — a rebuild is only worth it when the platform itself is holding you back.

What if I already run Google Ads?

Keep them running. SEO and ads answer different searches. The point of SEO is that you stop paying for the clicks you should already own, so the ad budget can go somewhere it earns more.

Which businesses is this for?

Melbourne trades and local service businesses — electricians, detailers, plumbers, installers. If your customers search by suburb and then call someone, this works.
