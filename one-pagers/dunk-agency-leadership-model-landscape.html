Alternative considered

A second senior leadership hire, either a General Manager or another Agency Lead, was considered. It is not recommended, for three reasons.
Reason 1

Role overlap

A General Manager or Agency Lead would own strategy, commercial and agency-wide initiatives. Ollie already holds those responsibilities, so the two roles would compete for the same remit.

Reason 2

$130k+

Base salary for a General Manager or Agency Lead, before on-costs. A Senior Digital Producer costs materially less.

Reason 3

Operational gap remains

The role does not cover resourcing, capacity planning or delivery coordination. A Senior Digital Producer would still be required.

Conclusion

A highly senior Agency Lead risks creating overlap with the strategic, commercial and agency-wide initiatives I am already positioned to own and have a clear vision for.

Instead, we can hire the operational capability we are actually missing, without creating competing ownership of agency direction.
