How this data was gathered, and where to treat it carefully

10–16 August comes from Google Search Console. 3–9 August comes from the Top Search Queries report in the old Wix dashboard, which draws on the same Google data.

The Wix export double-lists some terms and its rows do not add up to its own totals, so old-site term-level figures are indicative. The weekly totals — 1,211 appearances and 4 clicks — are reliable, and every percentage above is calculated from those. “New” means a term did not appear in last week's report, rather than that it was definitively zero.

Click volumes are small in absolute terms, so percentages will swing hard week to week until the numbers grow. The three reliable signals this week are the rise in appearances, the first non-brand clicks, and three service categories appearing that the old site was invisible for.
